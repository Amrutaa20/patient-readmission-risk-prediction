Search help

Search uses substring matching only within the fixed sample (dataset rows 0-49): row position; age group, gender, and race; medical specialty; admission, discharge, and source labels or codes; stay length, lab/procedure/medication counts, prior inpatient visits; and the recorded readmission code.

Examples: [50-60), Female, Caucasian, Emergency, SNF, or <30. Admission, discharge, and source labels come from the project's ID mapping file. Age, gender, and race are also available as dropdown filters.

Typing or changing dropdowns does not update results yet. Press Search, Enter, or Apply Filters to submit all current criteria.
